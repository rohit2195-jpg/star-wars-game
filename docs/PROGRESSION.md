# Progression System

## Philosophy

Inspired by Jedi: Fallen Order — progression is **ability-gated exploration** and **earned power**, not grinding. Roan gets stronger by finding new Force powers and upgrading existing ones through collectibles, not by farming experience points. The world opens up as abilities unlock.

No leveling system. No XP. Power comes from:
1. **Force powers** earned through story progression
2. **Upgrades** to those powers, found via exploration
3. **Health/Force pool** increases from hidden collectibles

---

## Force Power Unlock Order

Powers are unlocked in a fixed order tied to story progression. They are not optional — each is required to complete the chapter where it's introduced.

| Chapter | Power Unlocked | How Unlocked |
|---|---|---|
| Prologue | Force Push | Tutorial — Roan already has it |
| Chapter 1 | Force Pull | Environmental puzzle forces discovery |
| Chapter 2 | Force Lift | Door blocked by debris; puzzle forces it |
| Chapter 3 | Mind Trick | Imperial guard blocks a door; only solution |
| Chapter 3 | Slow Time | Falling debris section; death without it |
| Chapter 4 | Force Sight | Barrier room puzzle requires it |

All 6 powers are available for the final boss fight.

## Force Power Upgrade Tree

Each base power has **2 upgrades** found via collectibles (Holocron Fragments). Upgrades are **optional** — they improve the power but are never required to progress.

### Force Push

| Upgrade | Effect | Found |
|---|---|---|
| Extended Push | Push range +50%; can hit enemies 2 tiles further away | Ch. 2 optional zone |
| Concussive Push | Push staggers enemies for 20f longer; AoE radius increased | Ch. 4 revisit (Force Sight gate) |

### Force Pull

| Upgrade | Effect | Found |
|---|---|---|
| Pull Throw | Pulled objects can be immediately directed at enemies (hold direction) | Ch. 1 Sensor Tower |
| Double Pull | Pull two objects/enemies simultaneously | Ch. 3 Crystal Cavern |

### Force Lift

| Upgrade | Effect | Found |
|---|---|---|
| Sustained Lift | Can hold an object lifted for 60f instead of 30f | Ch. 2 Archive Wing |
| Lift Slam | Release lift downward to slam the object (deals 40 AoE damage) | Ch. 3 sealed door (Mind Trick gate) |

### Mind Trick

| Upgrade | Effect | Found |
|---|---|---|
| Extended Trick | Duration extended from 10s to 20s | Ch. 3 Side Passage |
| Group Trick | Can affect up to 2 targets simultaneously | Ch. 4 catacomb optional area |

### Slow Time

| Upgrade | Effect | Found |
|---|---|---|
| Extended Slow | Duration extended from 3s to 5s | Ch. 3 Trap Corridor secret |
| Combat Slow | Using Slow Time during a combo extends the combo window by 15f | Ch. 4 optional lore room |

### Force Sight

| Upgrade | Effect | Found |
|---|---|---|
| Sight Range | Detection radius increased; can sense enemies through walls | Ch. 4 inner sanctum |
| Sight Mark | Marked enemies (spotted via Sight) take +25% damage for 10s | Ch. 5 antechamber |

Found as **hidden collectibles** in each chapter — off the critical path but findable with exploration.

| Type | Effect | Total Available |

| Force Crystal (small) | +10 max HP | 5 across all chapters |

- HP: 200
- FP: 100

**Maximum (all upgrades found):**
- HP: 350
- FP: 175

## Collectibles

### Holocron Fragments

Small glowing cubes hidden off the critical path. Collecting them unlocks:
- Force power upgrades (see above)
- Lore entries in the pause menu journal
- A bonus lore scene if all are collected (post-game)

**Total:** 18 across all chapters  
**Per chapter:** 2–3

### Journal Pages

Documents left by the previous occupants of each location. Lore-only — no mechanical benefit. Completing a chapter's journal collection unlocks a bonus story entry.

**Total:** ~15 across all chapters

### Lightsaber Parts

Cosmetic only. Unlocks alternate hilt appearances for Roan's lightsaber. No gameplay effect.

**Total:** 6 (one per chapter, plus one in prologue)

### Force Echoes

Roan briefly experiences a vision of a past event at a significant location. Lore-only. Optional.

**Total:** 7 across all chapters

---

## Meditation Points (Save System)

Resting at a Meditation Point:
- **Saves progress** (checkpoint for respawn)
- **Restores** HP and FP to current maximum
- **Respawns** all standard enemies in the area (enemy encounters are intentionally non-permanent)
- **Does NOT respawn** mini-bosses or bosses

**Why respawn enemies?** Same design logic as JFO and Dark Souls — enemies are a resource for practicing combat and generating tension during revisits. Permanent enemy death makes the world feel static and removes the satisfaction of improving.

**Number per chapter:**
- Prologue: 1
- Chapter 1: 2
- Chapter 2: 2
- Chapter 3: 3
- Chapter 4: 2
- Chapter 5: 1

## New Game+ (Post-Launch / Later Milestone)

After completing the game, New Game+ unlocks:
- All Force powers from the start
- Enemies deal increased damage
- New Vaelen lore dialogue
- Alternate color for Roan's lightsaber (earned by the story choice made in Ch. 5)
