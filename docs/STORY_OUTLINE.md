# Story Outline

> **Status: Stub — gameplay takes priority. Flesh out details after core systems are built.**

## Setting & Era

**Time period:** The Golden Age of the Republic (approximately 200–500 BBY — well before the events of The Phantom Menace). The Sith have not been seen for over 1,000 years. The Jedi Order is at its height, at peace, and dangerously complacent about the Sith threat.

**Why this matters for gameplay:** The discovery of Sith evidence is not just a plot beat — it's a source of disbelief from the Order itself. Roan is investigating something the Council says doesn't exist. This creates moral tension woven into the level progression.

## Chapter-by-Chapter Summary

### Prologue — "The Signal"
**Setting:** Abandoned Jedi relay station on a remote moon

Roan responds alone to a faint distress signal from a derelict outpost — officially a routine check. While accessing a terminal deep in the station, rogue droids overwhelm him. He holds out as long as he can.

**Tutorial beat:** This teaches movement, basic combat (light/heavy attack, parry), and the opening Force Push.

**Rescue:** Pasha and Gaiana arrive — they intercepted his comms. Together they clear the station. But Roan found something before the droids hit: evidence of Sith activity. Not artifacts, not rumor — real, physical evidence. The group is shaken. Yoda is contacted via holo. The Council is skeptical but cannot ignore it.

**Chapter ends with:** Roan dispatched to investigate the source — the Outer Rim outpost where the signal originated.

---

### Chapter 1 — "Arrival"
**Setting:** Outer Rim moon exterior — craters, ruins, abandoned Imperial-era infrastructure

Roan arrives (with Pasha and Gaiana nearby but split up by a crash/attack). Solo platforming and droid combat. Reconnects with friends at end of chapter.

**Unlocks:** Force Pull

---

### Chapter 2 — "The Outpost"
**Setting:** Abandoned Jedi Outpost — collapsed corridors, dusty archives, damaged equipment

Roan explores the outpost. Meets Tazi, a local mechanic salvaging parts. She's hiding something. Force Lift puzzles to access sealed areas. Evidence of Sith presence grows stronger.

**Unlocks:** Force Lift

---

### Chapter 3 — "Below the Surface"
**Setting:** Underground ruins beneath the outpost — ancient construction predating the Republic

Environmental hazards, stealth sections (Imperial patrol found the moon too). Mind Trick introduced. Roan finds the first direct Sith artifact — not the holocron, but a trail that leads to it.

**Unlocks:** Mind Trick, Slow Time

---

### Chapter 4 — "The Lair"
**Setting:** Sith catacombs deeper underground

Darth Vaelen's presence is felt. Force shadow enemies resist normal attacks. Heavy combat. Pasha is injured and falls back. Gaiana stays with him. Roan presses alone.

**Unlocks:** Force Sight

---

### Chapter 5 — "The Holocron Chamber"
**Setting:** The deepest chamber — ancient Sith construction

Roan reaches the holocron. Darth Vaelen confronts him. Final boss fight. Emotional choice at the end: destroy the holocron (safe) or preserve it (the knowledge could help the Order understand the Sith threat — or corrupt the holder).

**The Rule of Two twist (optional lore):** The holocron predates the Rule of Two. Its contents could reveal how the Sith survived and why no one knew. The Council might not want this known.

## Tone Reference
- Humor comes from Roan's internal monologue and banter with Pasha/Gaiana/Tazi
- Maturity comes from the stakes: the Sith aren't supposed to exist anymore
- Story structure inspired by Jedi: Fallen Order — linear progression, emotional moments, a companion crew that grounds the player
- Cutscenes: comic-panel PNG strips (nod to the source material's art style)
