## Roan Novachez — Player Character

**Age:** 17–18  
**Weapon:** Single green lightsaber  
**Personality:** Confident but impulsive. Talks before thinking in social situations; acts before thinking in combat. Warm to his friends, stubborn with authority. Has grown since his Academy days — he's capable — but still has a habit of getting into situations bigger than he planned for.

**Player-facing mechanics:**
- Full movement: walk, run, jump, wall jump, roll/dash
- Light attack / heavy attack / combo strings
- Parry (blaster deflect + saber lock)
- All 6 Force powers (earned across chapters)
- Double jump (unlocked mid-game)

**Arc:** Starts as a confident young Knight who thinks this is a routine mission. Ends as someone who understands that the galaxy has dangers the Order refuses to see — and has to decide what to do with that knowledge.

## Pasha

**Role:** Roan's closest friend. Jedi Knight. Appears in the prologue rescue and travels with Roan through Chapter 4 (injured in Ch. 4, sits out Ch. 5).

**Personality:** Steady, dependable, dry humor. The one who plans while Roan charges in. Complements Roan without being a foil — they actually agree most of the time, which makes disagreements meaningful.

**In-game presence:**
- NPC companion in the field (not directly controlled)
- Assists in specific combat segments (scripted ally attacks)
- Source of banter during exploration
- His injury in Ch. 4 raises the emotional stakes of Ch. 5

**Gameplay hint:** Pasha's presence can be used to create "hold position" segments — Roan clears a path while Pasha holds off enemies at the rear.

---

## Gaiana

**Role:** Roan and Pasha's friend. Jedi Knight. Joins the prologue rescue; stays with Pasha after his injury in Ch. 4.

**Personality:** Perceptive and empathetic. More attuned to the Force than either of them. Often senses things Roan dismisses. Her concern about the Sith evidence is more spiritual than analytical — she feels something is wrong before they find proof.

**In-game presence:**
- NPC companion (less combat-focused than Pasha)
- Provides lore hints when near Force-sensitive artifacts
- Her worry functions as an emotional beat in Ch. 3 and 4

---

## Tazi

**Role:** Local mechanic and rebel sympathizer. Joins the group in Chapter 2. Comic relief but brilliant.

**Personality:** Talks too much, fixes everything. Has been on this moon longer than she should have — she knows its secrets but hasn't told anyone because no one asked. Doesn't trust Jedi at first ("you all have fancy swords and still crash your ships").

**In-game presence:**
- Opens locked areas (hacks terminals that Roan can't access with Force alone)
- Source of comic dialog during tense moments
- Knows the layout of the outpost and underground ruins from salvage runs
- Helps Pasha after his injury in Ch. 4

**Gameplay function:** Tazi acts as a "key" for puzzles that can't be Force-solved — certain doors, terminals, or sealed vaults. This prevents Force powers from being the solution to everything.

---

## Master Rykon

**Role:** Roan's archivist mentor at the Jedi Temple. Communicates only via holo. Stoic, precise, not warm.

**Personality:** Believes in the preservation of knowledge above all. Skeptical of Roan's impulsiveness but respects his instincts. The first person to take the Sith evidence seriously — and the first to warn Roan that the Council may not want it to be real.

**In-game presence:**
- Holo-call cutscenes between chapters (comic-panel style)
- Provides lore context for what Roan finds
- His concern grows as the chapters progress — foreshadows the Ch. 5 choice

---

## Yoda

**Role:** Jedi Council. Brief holo appearance after the Prologue. Sets the "investigate but do not engage" directive that Roan proceeds to ignore entirely.

**Personality:** Canon-accurate: gentle, wise, indirect. Not alarmed by Roan's findings — or chooses not to appear alarmed. "Dangerous, this path may be. But walk it alone, you must not."

**In-game presence:**
- One holo cutscene (Prologue → Chapter 1 transition)
- Optional holo terminal in Tazi's base (Ch. 2) where Roan can contact the Temple — Yoda's message is cryptic and unhelpful in the best way

---

## Darth Vaelen — Main Villain

**Role:** Original Sith character. The antagonist pursuing the holocron.

**Personality:** Calm. Patient. Does not monologue. More dangerous than he first appears — he's been operating in secret for decades without any Jedi sensing him. His willingness to let Roan get this far is deliberate.

**In-game presence:**
- First hinted in Ch. 1 (environmental signs — too deliberate to be coincidence)
- First seen in Ch. 3 briefly (Roan glimpses a figure — not yet a confrontation)
- Fully appears in Ch. 4 (sends shadow enemies, doesn't fight directly yet)
- Final boss in Ch. 5

## Companion Dynamics (Jedi Fallen Order Reference)

In Jedi: Fallen Order, Cal has a crew (Greez, Cere, BD-1) who ground him emotionally and provide gameplay utility. This game uses a similar structure:

| JFO Equivalent | This Game | Function |
|---|---|---|
| BD-1 (scanner/map) | Tazi (terminals/locks) | Gameplay utility + comic relief |
| Cere (mentor/history) | Master Rykon | Lore delivery + emotional weight |
| Greez (base/safety) | Pasha | Ground-level friendship + stakes |
| Second Force-sensitive | Gaiana | Emotional intuition + Force hints |
